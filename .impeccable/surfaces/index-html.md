## Direction contract

THESIS: The portrait is the site. One studio photograph continues into the page as its ground, and his name is set through it at full width. It refuses the split hero with a round avatar and the grid of equal project cards.

OWN-WORLD: Night navy taken from the studio backdrop (#090C18), cool white type (#ECEEF4), one azure accent (#4D8DFF). Geist at two extremes: very large, tight display lines and small quiet text. Hairline rules, 6px corners on media, pill-shaped controls. No cards, no decorative shadows, no icons.

STORY: The visitor sees who he is, reads in one line what he builds, watches four real projects arrive one over another with links to open them, learns his stack and schooling, and reaches one contact action.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Portrait centred at near full height, feathered into the ground on three sides and fading out below the chest. Name across the full width at about 8.5vw, over the fade. Intro line at the left and the four-project index at the right, both at about 22% height. Navigation along the top. Contact pill at bottom right.

FORM: The category standard (canon), user-chosen, portrait variant; position: the standing exit, not on the ordered list. Seed key 1b1930d2. Signature interaction: on scroll the portrait drifts and dims while the name rises through it, then the projects pin and stack, each sliding over the last. Motion grammar: masked line reveals, exponential ease-out, scrubbed parallax; everything off under reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

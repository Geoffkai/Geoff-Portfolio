## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static-first Vite build with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript modules, plus GSAP (ScrollTrigger) and Lenis for motion. No UI framework: the site is a handful of content pages where scroll choreography is the engineering, and it ships as static files to Vercel or GitHub Pages. Revisit only if the locked direction needs a component framework or WebGL scene graph.

## Users

People deciding whether to hire or contact Geoffrey, usually while comparing several candidates in a short sitting:

- Online freelance clients who find him through platforms such as Upwork, Fiverr or LinkedIn.
- Agencies and employers hiring a junior developer or contractor.
- Startup founders who need a landing page, product site or MVP built.

Their job: decide in seconds whether he is worth a closer look, then check real projects for proof before making contact.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Geoffrey Adam Tomagan, a third-year BS Computer Science student at the University of the Philippines Tacloban (confirmed 2026-10-08). It exists to win freelance work and junior roles. Success is a visitor who understands what he builds, opens at least one working project, and contacts him.

## Positioning

He wants to be hired for, in his words on 2026-10-08: websites and landing pages, full-stack web apps, fixing existing sites, and software developer or software engineering roles. The site presents him as an emerging developer, not a specialist.

Working position, inferred from the projects and not yet confirmed by him: range across the stack, shown through four shipped things of four different kinds (a full-stack web app, a browser extension, an algorithmic web game, a desktop database app).

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from a freelance profile, a LinkedIn or GitHub link, or a job application. Many are on a phone. They scan, open one or two projects, and leave or reach out. Live demos and source repositories are what they evaluate.

Two question categories, five levels, character selection, score records, sound, JSON question banks, Maven build, Launch4j executable.

## Product Principles

1. The work is the proof. Every claim points at something a visitor can open and use.
2. Honest about career stage. A student and emerging developer, with no inflated titles, clients or numbers.
3. Fast to a verdict. A busy client knows what he builds and how to reach him within seconds.
4. Range is the story. Four different kinds of software, each shown as itself.
5. One clear way to make contact, repeated consistently.
